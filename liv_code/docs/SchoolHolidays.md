### Two important weather-related closures

There were **at least three distinct emergency-weather episodes** worth separating from ordinary holidays:

**1. January — cold wave/fog**
This was the biggest winter disruption. Lucknow repeatedly extended the Class 1–8 closure, ultimately reaching Jan 16/17 in the reporting. The administration cited low temperatures, cold-wave conditions and fog, while requiring later classes to use safer 10 AM–3 PM timings. ([India Today][2])

**2. August 8 — heavy rain/waterlogging**
Lucknow closed government, aided and private schools **up to Class 8** on August 8 because of heavy rainfall, waterlogging and a forecast of additional rain. ([Moneycontrol][11])

**3. August 14 — heavy rain/adverse weather**
This was broader: **Classes 1–12** in government and private schools were closed across Lucknow on August 14 because of torrential rain, waterlogging and associated transport/safety problems. ([Amar Ujala][8])

### What about the regular holidays?

The UP government's 2025 calendar listed **24 public holidays and 31 restricted holidays**, although 14 of the holidays fell on weekends. The main public/festival dates relevant to schools included Maha Shivratri, Holi, Eid-ul-Fitr, Ram Navami, Mahavir Jayanti, Ambedkar Jayanti, Good Friday, Buddha Purnima, Bakrid, Independence Day, Janmashtami, Eid-e-Milad, Dussehra/Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali, Govardhan Puja, Bhai Dooj, Guru Nanak Jayanti and Christmas. ([The Times of India][5])

So, **if your question is specifically "what caused an unscheduled closure in Lucknow in 2025?"**, rather than "what were all school holidays?", the answer is essentially:

1. **Severe winter/cold wave + fog — January**
2. **Extreme summer heat/heatwave — June extension**
3. **Heavy monsoon rain + waterlogging — August 8**
4. **Heavy monsoon rain + adverse weather/waterlogging — August 14**
5. **Severe winter/cold wave + dense fog — December 24–31**

The rest were predominantly **pre-scheduled festival/national/summer/winter vacations**, and exact dates varied by school.

One caveat: **private schools in Lucknow did not necessarily follow every UP-government holiday identically**. For example, individual Lucknow schools publish their own academic calendars and can modify dates subject to government declarations. ([lucknowpublicschoolsandcolleges.com][12])
